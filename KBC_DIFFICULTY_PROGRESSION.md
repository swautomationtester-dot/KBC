# GamesArena KBC Difficulty Progression

Every 5-question KBC game now follows a fixed difficulty curve:

- Q1 — ₹10: Kids General Knowledge — Very Easy
- Q2 — ₹20: Kids General Knowledge — Easy
- Q3 — ₹30: Indian Politics / Polity — Medium (easy/moderate bank only)
- Q4 — ₹40: Technology — Hard
- Q5 — ₹50: South Indian Cinema — Very Hard

Q1 and Q2 always start with Kids General Knowledge. Q4 and Q5 deliberately become harder. Hard political questions and Bollywood/Indian Cinema questions remain excluded. Existing room-level used-question tracking prevents repeats until the bank is exhausted/reset.
